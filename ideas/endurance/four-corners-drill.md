# Four Corners Drill

**Equipment:** 4 cones
**Time Cap:** 10-15 minutes
**Scaling:** Reduce to 3 sets, increase square size to 15x15 yards for beginners

**Workout:**
1. Set up 4 cones in a 10x10 yard square
2. Starting at cone 1:
   - Backpedal to cone 2 (parallel cone)
   - Shuffle to cone 3 (feet don't cross or touch)
   - Karaoke to cone 4
   - Sprint to cone 1 (starting position)
3. Complete 5 sets
4. Rest 1 minute between sets

**Notes:**
Mix up movements to keep it challenging: bear crawl, high knees, butt kicks. Focus on proper movement mechanics - quality over speed for technique movements.
