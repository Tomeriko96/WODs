# 50 40s

**Equipment:** Markers for 40-yard distance
**Time Cap:** 25-30 minutes
**Scaling:** Start with 30 40s, reduce rest to 30-45 seconds for advanced

**Workout:**
- Sprint 40 yards
- Rest 1-2 minutes
- Repeat for 50 total sprints

**Notes:**
Simple but brutal sprint conditioning. Mark off exactly 40 yards and focus on maximum effort each sprint. Track your times to monitor consistency - try not to drop off significantly by the end.
