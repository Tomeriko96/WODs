# 100 Burpee Challenge

**Equipment:** None
**Time Cap:** 20 minutes
**Scaling:** Reduce to 5-8 burpees per set, or do step-back burpees

**Workout:**
- 10 sets of 10 burpees
- 1 minute rest between each set

**Notes:**
A classic bodyweight endurance test. Focus on consistent pacing - don't go too hard in early sets. Maintain good burpee form throughout: chest to floor, full hip extension at the top.
