# The Gut Buster

**Equipment:** 2 cones, 40 yards of space
**Time Cap:** 20-25 minutes
**Scaling:** Reduce reps to 5-8 per exercise, reduce sprint distance to 20-30 yards

**Workout:**
1. Set up two cones 40 yards apart
2. At cone 1: 10 squat jumps
3. Sprint to cone 2
4. At cone 2: 10 sit-ups
5. Sprint back to cone 1: 10 up-downs (burpees)
6. Sprint to cone 2: 10 push-ups
7. Sprint to cone 1: 10 mountain climbers
8. Sprint to cone 2: 10 butt kickers
9. Sprint to cone 1: 10 jumping jacks
10. Sprint to cone 2: 10 push-ups
11. Sprint 40 yards to finish

**Notes:**
This was a favorite high school football conditioning drill. Pace yourself - it's tougher than it looks! Focus on maintaining good form even when fatigued.
